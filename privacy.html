X7 Kids Academy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

X7 Kids Academy is designed as an offline-first educational experience for children. A child does not need a Google, Apple, social-media or X7 account to use the core learning experience.

Data stored on the device

The app may store a parent-created child nickname, age band, learning progress, rewards, Parent PIN verification data and local Learning Lab entitlement state on the device. The age is used to select age-appropriate learning content.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not include advertising SDKs, behavioural advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Purchases

Optional Learning Lab subscriptions are available only through the parent-gated store. Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. Store providers may process purchase and account information under their own privacy policies. X7 does not ask a child to enter payment information.

Children's privacy

The child gameplay area does not include chat, social posting, user-generated public content or external advertising links. Parent controls protect purchase and parent-only areas.

Deletion

Local app data can be removed by resetting app data or uninstalling the app. Store subscription records are managed by the user's device store account.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or store requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
